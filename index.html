🛡 Trading Guide

A few simple ways to trade safer.
  1. Check both fruits' values before agreeing.
  2. Confirm current stock in the game.
  3. Use official in-game trade windows.
  4. Never share Roblox passwords or verification codes.
  5. Report suspicious offers to community moderators.
